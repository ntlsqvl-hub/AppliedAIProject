# State case-study sources: cost, political, and administrative feasibility

Textual sources (GAO reports, legislative history, program documentation)
supporting the memo's qualitative case studies, gathered to pair with the
quantitative state-level data in `aggregated/`. Six states were researched in
parallel by separate subagents, each instructed to only cite sources it could
verify were real — sources it couldn't confirm are noted as gaps, not
guessed at. Pulled 2026-10-15.

Two groups, based on the funding-vs-vulnerability pattern in
`aggregated/openfema_funding_by_state_2017_2026.csv` joined against
`aggregated/cejst_by_state.csv` / `aggregated/ejscreen_by_state.csv`:
- **High-vulnerability, high-funding**: Louisiana, Kentucky, Mississippi
- **High-vulnerability, low-funding**: Oklahoma, West Virginia, Arkansas

---

## Louisiana

1. **DHS OIG, "Louisiana Did Not Properly Oversee a $706.6 Million Hazard Mitigation Grant Program Award for Work on Louisiana Homes,"** Report OIG-19-54 (Jul. 25, 2019). https://www.oversight.gov/reports/louisiana-did-not-properly-oversee-7066-million-hazard-mitigation-grant-program-award-work (PDF: https://www.oversight.gov/sites/default/files/oig-reports/OIG-19-54-Jul19.pdf) — *Administrative complexity, cost/fiscal feasibility.* LA couldn't document $79.7M in payments and drew $50.4M more than obligated.
2. **GAO-09-541, "Gulf Coast Disaster Recovery: CDBG Program Guidance to States Needs to Be Improved"** (Jun. 19, 2009). https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-09-541 — *Administrative complexity, cost/fiscal feasibility.* Covers Road Home program's rollout difficulties.
3. **CRS Report RL34410, "The Louisiana Road Home Program"** (Mar. 10, 2008). https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/RL34410.html — *Cost/fiscal feasibility, administrative complexity.* LA received 68% ($13.41B) of the $19.7B Gulf Coast CDBG-DR total; documents multi-agency (FEMA/HUD) jurisdictional conflicts.
4. **Louisiana Legislative Auditor, HMGP reimbursement performance audit, Jul–Dec 2022** (2023). https://lla.la.gov/publicreports.nsf/0/b6f0bb2a2d05ed478625898e006a7e62/$file/00001254.pdf — *Administrative complexity, cost/fiscal feasibility.* 14.6% of $31.4M reviewed lacked adequate documentation.
5. **LA congressional delegation letters on FEMA PA cost-share adjustment after Hurricane Laura** (Sept–Oct 2020). Reps. Higgins, Johnson; Sen. Kennedy. https://clayhiggins.house.gov/media/press-releases/higgins-requests-cost-share-adjustment-fema-public-assistance-funds ; https://mikejohnson.house.gov/media/press-releases/johnson-urges-white-house-adjust-cost-share-hurricane-laura-recovery — *Political feasibility, cost/fiscal feasibility.* Secured cost-share reduction from 25% to 10% state match via Stafford Act §1232/DRRA authority.
6. **Louisiana Office of Community Development, "Non-Federal Cost Share Match Program" guidance** (post-2016 floods). https://watershed.la.gov/non-federal-cost-share-program ; https://doa.la.gov/media/ccpncuvg/iin-2018-1-ocd-dru-cdbg-match-fund-insurance-information.pdf — *Cost/fiscal feasibility.* $105M in CDBG-DR used to cover LA's own FEMA match.

*Not verified / excluded:* a rumored "GAO January 2010" report on Road Home renter/homeowner disparities could not be confirmed on gao.gov.

## Kentucky

1. **GAO-22-104039, "Disaster Recovery: Additional Actions Needed to Identify and Address Potential Recovery Barriers"** (Dec. 15, 2021). https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-22-104039 — *Administrative complexity.* National (not KY-specific): rural/low-capacity communities lack contractor capacity large cities have; most programs don't collect demographic data on applicants.
2. **FEMA Press Release HQ-22-090, major disaster declaration for Kentucky (DR-4663-KY)** (Jul. 29, 2022). https://www.fema.gov/press-release/20220729/president-joseph-r-biden-jr-approves-major-disaster-declaration-kentucky — *Cost/fiscal feasibility.*
3. **KY Dept. of Military Affairs, Division of Emergency Management, "SFY2023 Necessary Government Expenditures"** (presentation to KY General Assembly). https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/CommitteeDocuments/10/26554/2.%20Military%20Affairs%20Update.pdf — *Cost/fiscal feasibility, administrative complexity.* $260.2M of $273.2M total state disaster spend went to the 2022 flood; documents cost-share step-downs (100%→75/25%) and that FEMA obligation "may take a few months or well over a year."
4. **KY Public Protection Cabinet, "Team Eastern Kentucky Flood Relief Fund — Program Guidelines."** https://ppc.ky.gov/Documents/Team%20EKY%20Flood%20Relief%20Fund%20Program%20Guidelines.pdf — *Cost/fiscal feasibility.* State-run parallel fund created because FEMA/IA alone didn't cover gaps.
5. **KY General Assembly, 2022 Third Extraordinary Session (HB1/SB1), creating EKSAFE fund (~$213M)**, signed Aug. 26, 2022. Via KY LRC "Interim Record": https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/record/interim_records/Interim_Sep22.pdf — *Political feasibility, cost/fiscal feasibility.* Unanimous special-session bipartisan action. **Caveat: verify exact dollar breakdown against the enrolled bill before citing specifics — secondary sources gave inconsistent figures.**

*Not verified / excluded:* no KY-flood-specific GAO report found; no confirmed McConnell-specific primary document (only secondary claims via Rep. Rogers's office); a 2025 KY LRC flood task force memo exists but its text couldn't be extracted/confirmed.

## Mississippi

1. **GAO-09-541** (see Louisiana #2) — also covers Mississippi's CDBG allocation shift from 63% to 52% housing share toward economic development.
2. **GAO-10-17, "Disaster Assistance: Federal Assistance for Permanent Housing Primarily Benefited Homeowners..."** (Oct. 2009). https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-10-17 — *Administrative complexity, cost/fiscal feasibility.* MS/LA CDBG reached 62% of damaged homeowner units but only 18% of damaged rental units ($13B vs. $1.8B) — direct documented equity gap.
3. **HUD OIG, Audit Report 2009-AT-1802, Mississippi Homeowner Assistance Program Phase II** (2009). https://www.hudoig.gov/sites/default/files/documents/audit-reports/ig09111801.pdf — *Administrative complexity, cost/fiscal feasibility.* MS's $5.058B CDBG-DR Katrina allocation and Phase II eligibility review.
4. **Haley Barbour, testimony before Senate HSGAC.** https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/imo/media/doc/BarbourTestimony.pdf — *Cost/fiscal feasibility, political feasibility.*
5. **House Oversight Committee, Thompson/Maloney press release requesting GAO review of federal water-utility disaster support** (Nov. 18, 2022). https://oversightdemocrats.house.gov/news/press-releases/chairs-thompson-maloney-request-gao-conduct-review-of-the-federal-government-s — *Political feasibility.* Prompted by the Jackson water crisis.

*Not verified / excluded:* a widely-reported ~$132M HUD civil-rights settlement (Mississippi Center for Justice v. HUD) over disparate treatment of Black wind-damaged homeowners — reported by NPR/wire services but the primary settlement document/docket could not be located. **Worth chasing directly** — it's exactly the disparate-treatment evidence the memo wants. A Senate Appropriations hearing (S.Hrg. 109-752, Mar. 2006, Cochran chairing) on the FY2006 Katrina supplemental is a strong lead but unconfirmed on congress.gov/govinfo.gov in this pass.

## Oklahoma

1. **GAO-21-140, "Disaster Resilience: FEMA Should Take Additional Steps to Streamline Hazard Mitigation Grants..."** (Feb. 2, 2021). https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-21-140 — *Administrative complexity.* National: 10 of 12 jurisdictions called HMA applications "complex and lengthy."
2. **GAO-26-107774, "FEMA: Billions in Building Resilient Infrastructure and Communities Subgrants Remain Unawarded"** (Sept. 24, 2026). https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-26-107774/index.html — *Administrative complexity.* Only 1,245 of eligible BRIC subgrants awarded FY2020-23; quotes FEMA's own 2023 stakeholder report that consultant-dependency "may be inequitable for small, rural, and disadvantaged communities."
3. **Oklahoma Dept. of Emergency Management, "Mitigation" program page.** https://oklahoma.gov/oem/programs-and-services/mitigation.html — *Administrative complexity.* No open HMGP opportunities as of Oct. 2026; thin grant pipeline.
4. **Gaylord News / Black Wall Street Times, "FEMA restructuring could leave out Tornado Alley"** (Jul. 7, 2025). https://theblackwallsttimes.com/2025/07/07/fema-restructuring-could-leave-out-tornado-alley/ — *Political feasibility, cost/fiscal context.* OK received only ~$447M in FEMA payments since 2020; no Tornado Alley seat on the FEMA Review Council.
5. **Office of Sen. James Lankford, press release on OK delegation disaster-declaration request** (May 1, 2024). https://www.lankford.senate.gov/?p=15157 — *Political feasibility.* Full delegation + three tribal nations requested a declaration; reactive rather than structural engagement.

*Not verified / excluded:* no GAO report specific to Oklahoma or its tribal nations' hazard mitigation; no Oklahoma-specific BRIC/HMGP application-vs-award counts found via web search (would need a direct OpenFEMA query).

## West Virginia

1. **WV Legislative Auditor, "RISE West Virginia Flood Recovery Program Update"** (Dec. 9, 2018). https://www.wvlegislature.gov/legisdocs/reports/agency/PA/PA_2018_657.pdf — *Administrative complexity.* Zero of 49 planned manufactured-housing rebuilds completed under the $150M HUD-funded RISE WV program.
2. **WV Legislature, Joint Committee on Flooding, interim presentation** (Jul. 12, 2018). https://www.wvlegislature.gov/legisdocs/2018/committee/interim/JLCF/JLCF_20180712204533.pdf — *Cost/fiscal feasibility, administrative complexity.* Only ~$1.4M of $149M CDBG-DR expended at time of presentation. **Caveat: figures from secondary summary, not directly extracted — re-confirm before citing.**
3. **HUD OIG / DOJ, Horne LLP False Claims Act settlement ($1,207,600)**. https://hudoig.gov/newsroom/press-release/mississippi-firm-pay-1207600-resolve-disaster-recovery-claims — *Administrative complexity.* Contractor hired to write WV's CDBG-DR plan settled claims over falsified field data.
4. **Sens. Capito and Manchin et al., letter to HUD Secretary Castro** (Oct. 6, 2016). https://www.capito.senate.gov/news/press-releases/delegation-urges-hud-to-allocate-funding-to-west-virginia-flood-recovery — *Political feasibility.* Full delegation lobbied for "a fair portion" of CDBG-DR funds.
5. **Headwaters Economics, "FEMA's BRIC Program Continues to Fund Innovative Risk Reduction but Community Capacity Limits Access"** (2023). https://headwaterseconomics.org/natural-hazards/bric-program-capacity/ — *Administrative complexity.* National: only 3% of round-three BRIC funding went to low-capacity counties vs. 83% to high-capacity counties.
6. **GAO, RCED-88-5, "Disaster Assistance"** (1988, review of 1985 WV flood). https://www.gao.gov/assets/rced-88-5.pdf — *Administrative complexity (historical precedent only — predates BRIC).*

*Not verified / excluded:* no GAO report specifically auditing WV's 2016 flood/RISE WV recovery; no GAO report specifically critiquing BRIC's benefit-cost-ratio on equity grounds (GAO-26-107774 covers timelines, not equity, for WV's purposes).

## Arkansas

1. **GAO-21-140** (see Oklahoma #1) — same national finding, applied here as context for AR.
2. **Headwaters Economics, "Capacity-limited states still struggle to access FEMA BRIC grants"** (Aug. 4, 2022). https://headwaterseconomics.org/equity/capacity-limited-fema-bric-grants/ — *Administrative complexity.* 94% of FY2020 BRIC funding went to coastal/wealthier states; only 19 states received any FY2021 national-competition award.
3. **Arkansas Division of Emergency Management, Hazard Mitigation Grants page.** https://dps.arkansas.gov/emergency-management/adem/grants-funding/hazard-mitigation-grants/ — *Cost/fiscal feasibility, administrative complexity.* Confirms flat 75/25 state/local match with no low-income waiver; narrow annual application windows.
4. **H.R. 4669, "Fixing Emergency Management for Americans (FEMA) Act of 2025"** (ordered reported 57-3, Sept. 3, 2025). https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hr4669ih/xhtml/BILLS-119hr4669ih.html — *Political feasibility.* AR's Rep. Westerman actively engaged on House T&I Committee.
5. **Rep. Bruce Westerman, weekly column, "Protecting Arkansas Communities in the Wake of Natural Disasters"** (Sept. 5, 2025). https://westerman.house.gov/media-center/weekly-columns/protecting-arkansas-communities-wake-natural-disasters — *Political feasibility.* On-record criticism that current disaster-relief processes "are not working the way they should."
6. **Rep. Rick Crawford, press release on preserving FEMA Disaster Relief Fund appropriations** (Jun. 2, 2011). https://crawford.house.gov/media/press-releases/congressman-crawford-preserves-fema-funding-for-first-district — *Political feasibility (historical baseline, predates BRIC).*

*Not verified / excluded:* no GAO report names Arkansas specifically; no Arkansas-specific BRIC/HMGP application-vs-award counts found (would need a direct OpenFEMA query or FOIA).

## Cross-cutting gaps for the memo to flag

- The "low-capacity states get disadvantaged by BRIC" argument rests on **national** GAO/Headwaters Economics evidence applied to Oklahoma, West Virginia, and Arkansas — no state-specific GAO audit exists for any of the three.
- State-specific BRIC/HMGP application-vs-award counts for Oklahoma and Arkansas aren't publicly searchable as text; a direct OpenFEMA API query (same method used in `aggregate.R`) could fill this gap quantitatively.
- A Mississippi HUD civil-rights settlement over disparate treatment of Black wind-damaged homeowners is reported in secondary sources but unverified here — the single most promising unverified lead for the equity argument specifically.
