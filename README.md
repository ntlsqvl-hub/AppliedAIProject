# AppliedAIProject
### Policy question: How can federal disaster-recovery and restoration mechanisms address the disproportionate harm that extreme weather events impose on marginalized communities in the United States?
Audience: State/local emergency management officials and legislators deciding how to allocate climate-resilience and disaster-recovery funding.
Proposed data source: OpenFEMA on where federal disaster funding goes, mirrors of EJScreen and CEJST data.
Intended product: decision memo with technical appendix.
